Decisions

IDChoice · Rationale
D-001Use node:22-bookworm-slim. The app requires Node 20+ and slim Debian avoids Alpine native-package surprises.
D-002Expose 8080. DigitalOcean defaults to that public HTTP port.
D-003Do not copy desktop/ or test/. Hosted backend does not need Electron or tests at runtime.
D-004Sync package-lock.json before deploy. DigitalOcean runs npm ci --omit=dev and rejects stale peer entries.

Tradeoffs

IDPicked over Rejected · Reason
T-001Root Dockerfile over buildpack auto-detect. App Platform can pick a deterministic container without guessing Node settings.

Reversibility

R-001 · medium. Delete Dockerfile and .dockerignore or switch App Platform back to buildpack.
