Decisions

IDChoice · Rationale
D-001Choose lifecycle before provider. The durable workspace contract matters more than Daytona versus VPS.
D-002Recommend persistent Daytona workspace per user + project first. Existing integration gives isolation and previews with lower ops burden.
D-003Keep per-task fresh sandboxes as clean-room mode. Reproducible and risky tasks still need disposable environments.
D-004Treat per-user/project VPS as provider v2. It wins on devbox feel but adds provisioning, patching, networking, and recovery work.
D-005Reject one shared DigitalOcean box/container pool for MVP. Cross-user noise and isolation risk hide the real cost.

IDPicked over Rejected · Reason
T-001Persistent workspace over Dockerfile automation first. Persisting installs solves the user pain before modeling every dependency.
T-002Provider adapter over Daytona rewrite. The product UX can stay stable while infrastructure changes later.
