Algorithm

  1. Score each candidate with weighted criteria: total = sum(score_i * weight_i).
  2. For each round, keep the top genes and mutate under-scored criteria into ten new candidates.
  3. Stop after five ten-candidate rounds and implement the highest-scoring final concept.

UI layout

  1. Loop report: show rubric, round winners, every candidate score, and transition notes.
  2. Final page: lead with portable local Pi on remote agents and support it with recurring, env, cloud-loop, and evidence proof.
