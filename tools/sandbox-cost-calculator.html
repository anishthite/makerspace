Model notes: Sprites bill compute only while CPU is active (idle = storage only). E2B, Daytona, Modal, Northflank and Morph bill wall-clock while the sandbox is alive — aggressive pause/resume between turns can cut those toward the active-share number, at the cost of harness complexity. Vercel and Cloudflare bill active CPU but provisioned RAM for the full session (Cloudflare adds provisioned disk while alive, plus small Workers/Durable Objects charges not modeled). E2B Pro fee kicks in automatically here when sessions > 1 hr or peak concurrency > 20.
