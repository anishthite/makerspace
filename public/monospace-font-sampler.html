quick notes

Use one font, keep fallbacks.

ship Use Hack for now.
alternate Use IBM Plex Mono if we want a warmer, less IDE-ish tone.
avoid Avoid display-heavy options for tiny status labels.
skip Paid faces like Berkeley Mono need a license before they are real options.
